the Z-Biomechanics armature (flattened into a plain bone chain), skin-weighted,
and packed with gltfpack. The scripts that do it are in pipeline/ of this repo
(`just anatomy`). No non-commercial parts of the Z-Anatomy source (inner ear,
kidney) are included; the pipeline's deny list refuses them.
